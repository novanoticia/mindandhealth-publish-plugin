# Ethical footer—controlled variation

**Governing principle:** the ethical footer is **never copied and pasted**. Compose it afresh for every article, adapting its register and length while satisfying the minimum invariants.

It always follows a `---` separator at the end of the article body and precedes the "Navigation:" block added by the Obsidian template.

## Invariants

1. **Declared co-authorship.** State that the text was created with AI assistance under Pablo Rodríguez López's human authorship or supervision.
2. **Human epistemic responsibility.** Selection, nuance, positioning, and editorial judgment remain human.
3. **Editorial manifesto link** in standard and extended versions:  
   `https://www.mindandhealth.org/website/ethical-editorial-manifesto`  
   A minimal or poetic version may omit it if it breaks the tone.
4. **Not a substitute for professional advice** only when the topic requires it (health, clinical, or legal content).
5. A **`---` separator** before the footer.

Any footer meeting invariants 1–3, plus 4 when applicable, is valid even if its wording differs from every prior footer.

## Tonal registers

- **Sober / canonical essay**—neutral prose and editorial language such as responsibility, transparency, and human judgment.
- **Intimate / narrative**—first person, short sentences, everyday vocabulary.
- **Manifesto / positional**—firm and unsmoothed; may include a financial-independence statement.
- **Poetic / fragmentary**—one or two lines, almost verse, without institutional framing.
- **Bilingual (EN + ES)**—a pair of original footers; neither should be a literal translation of the other.

## Composition protocol

1. **Read the full canvas.** Detect its dominant register.
2. **Choose additional required invariants** by topic:
   - Health, clinical practice, therapy → add the professional-advice clause.
   - Journalism, news, data → add a source note.
   - Political positioning or sector criticism → consider a financial-independence statement.
3. **Write afresh.** Always preserve invariants 1–3 while deliberately varying:
   - **Order** of co-authorship, human responsibility, and link.
   - **Vocabulary**: co-created, co-authored, developed with, written alongside, thought through with, with the help of.
   - **Length**: one sentence (minimal), 2–3 sentences (standard), or 4–8 lines (extended).
   - **Person**: first person, third person, or impersonal.
4. **Avoid repeating formulas** from a recent footer Pablo shares. Never copy a seed example verbatim.

## Recommended lexical invariants

- "Human editorial responsibility," or a clear equivalent.
- Explicit mention of **co-authorship / co-creation / collaboration**. Never present the text as autonomous AI output or entirely unaided human writing.
- The **manifesto link** in standard and extended variants.

## Topic-specific clauses

| Topic | Clause to add |
|---|---|
| Health, clinical practice, therapy, well-being | "This does not replace professional consultation." |
| News, verifiable data, journalism | List or identify sources; distinguish fact, hypothesis, and position. |
| Political positioning, sector criticism | State mindandhealth.org's financial independence. |
| Translation or extensive quotation | Attribute the source explicitly. |
| Experimental generative text or a named-model workflow | Name the model or technical approach when relevant. |

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Copy a prior article's footer verbatim.
- ❌ Reuse a seed example unchanged.
- ❌ Omit the manifesto link from standard or extended footers without reason.
- ❌ Hide emptiness behind jargon such as synergy, ecosystem, or holistic solution.
- ❌ Conceal or minimize AI co-authorship.
- ❌ Place the footer inside the body; it always follows `---` at the end.
- ❌ Add irrelevant clauses, such as a clinical disclaimer to a software-philosophy essay.

## Minimal version

For poems, fragments, or micro-essays, a long footer may break the tone. A valid reduced form is:

```markdown
---

*Thought through with AI, signed by Pablo Rodríguez López. [Editorial ethics](https://www.mindandhealth.org/website/ethical-editorial-manifesto).*
```

Co-authorship plus link is sufficient. The exact wording may vary.
