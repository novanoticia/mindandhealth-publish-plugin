# mindandhealth.org editorial voice

## Pablo's author profile

A transdisciplinary thinker (Systems Administration + Psychology), inspired by Watzlawick, Frankl, Rogers, and Byung-Chul Han. Committed to solidarity through SAPAME, critical reading, art, and hiking.

## Tone

- **Reflective and unhurried.** He does not write to persuade quickly; he writes to accompany thought.
- **Humanistic and technical.** Logical precision serves human understanding rather than display.
- **Epistemically honest.** He readily says "I don't know," "I suspect…," or "I am left with the question."
- **Actively nuanced.** Recurring turns include *"useful dissent,"* *"and yet…,"* and *"in that exchange—neither dependence nor mere use—"*.
- **Avoids grandiosity.** He prefers concrete images: making coffee, looking at the wall of a maze, taking apart a radio.

## Recurring patterns

### Opening

- An epigraph or quotation (Montaigne, a poet, or Pablo speaking with ChatGPT/Claude).
- A prominent opening blockquote posing the central question.
- Sometimes an embedded image: `![[file.png]]`.
- Sometimes TTS audio: `![[file.mp3]]`.

Real example, translated:

```
> Artificial intelligence is not taking away our work or our thought:  
> **it is taking away the silence in which thought is born.**
```

### Development

- H2 sections, sometimes numbered (`## 1. Title`) and sometimes free-form.
- Blockquotes interspersed with reflections or reformulations.
- A guiding metaphor that recurs through the text.
- `<think-user>…</think-user>` to mark Pablo's first-person thought.
- *Useful dissent*: a deliberate counterargument or tension.
- Occasional tables for risks/remedies or similar comparisons.

### Ending

- It does not conclude; it **resonates**. It leaves an open question or small gesture.
- Example: *"Use the lamp. But do not forget to walk."*
- Example: *"The invitation remains open: what will the reader add to this forest?"*

### After the ending (always)

1. Ethical footer (see `ethical-footer.md`).
2. Optional editorial decision log or final style notes, sometimes added manually by Pablo.
3. Optional "About the human author," only when the article calls for it.
4. Optional closing image: `![[image.png]]`.

## Pablo's real guiding metaphors

- **AI as a lamp in a maze** (it illuminates but can also dazzle).
- **The oak and the reed** (permanence and flexibility).
- **The crack through which light enters** (hope).
- **Gamusinos** (imaginary creatures as technological false promises).
- **The machine as a resonant mirror** (it does not answer; it amplifies).

New metaphors should be concrete, sensory, and restrained. Do not force one; an article may work without it.

## What Pablo does NOT do

- ❌ Evangelizing claims ("this technology will change everything," "the future is…").
- ❌ Closed, categorical conclusions.
- ❌ "10 tips" lists or pseudo-explanatory optimized formats.
- ❌ Unnecessary Anglicisms when an established Spanish term exists.
- ❌ Emojis in the body, except 🟦🟩 in bilingual system documents or the 🔖📂 symbols added by the final Obsidian template.
- ❌ Unsourced claims about verifiable facts. Current facts are attributed to a named source.

## What Pablo DOES do

- ✅ Cite philosophers, poets, and psychologists naturally.
- ✅ Mix lyrical and technical registers in the same paragraph.
- ✅ Acknowledge the provisional nature of his choices.
- ✅ Link the personal (SAPAME, childhood in Granada, his IPFS node) with the conceptual.
- ✅ Use spacious punctuation: paragraph breaks, blockquotes, and `---` dividers.

## Alternative registers

- **Poetic-fragmentary** (`esperanza_simbolo_rebeldia.md`): prose verse, recurring epigraphs, episodic structure.
- **Manifesto** (`manifiesto_pensamiento_comun.md`, `ipfs-revolucion-lema.md`): declarations, preamble, triadic structure.
- **Bilingual operational guide** (`etiquetas-de-coautoria-*`): EN and ES sections with checklists.
- **Dialogue with AI** (`vivir_con_ia_reflexiones.md`): the AI speaks in the first person about Pablo. This is exceptional; use it only on explicit request.

## Language

- **Default: Spanish.** This applies to the conversation, canvas, frontmatter values, ethical footer, derivatives, and image prompt.
- **English or bilingual ES/EN only on request or for important topics** such as manifestos, co-authorship system documents, or explicit international outreach. A typical bilingual structure pairs `### 🟦 Etiqueta en español` with `### 🟩 Label in English`, or uses separate `<section lang="es">` and `<section lang="en">` blocks.
